1 · A section heading, numbered

Body text is Source Sans 3 at 10 point. Write plainly: short sentences, the reader's words, one idea in bold where it carries the paragraph, and never a register id in anything that leaves the building.

  • A bullet with a lead-in. The marker is amber; a numbered list's is plum.
  • A second bullet, plain.

A sub-heading, when a section needs one

  1. A1A numbered question the reader can answer by its id, as the bank letters do.

A note is for the one thing the reader must not miss: a deadline, a condition, a decision we need.
