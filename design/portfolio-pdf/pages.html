Who we are

One technology partner, not one product

Dibon Consulting builds the software that banks, Saccos, microfinance institutions, payment providers and the businesses around them use to collect, lend, hold and reconcile money. Payments, deposit-taking, lending, escrow and reconciliation come together with monitoring, messaging, identity and ERP integration, so an institution works with one partner rather than five.

Mission

Driving scalable business expansion through immersive, customised software solutions.

Vision

Shaping the future through transformative partnerships, harnessing emerging technologies and world-class resources.

Values

Innovation, a client-centric approach, excellence, collaboration, integrity, continuous learning, agility and social responsibility.

The problems we solve

Where money gets lost in the work

Reconciliation by hand

Statements downloaded from every bank and mobile-money portal, matched line by line. Breaks found late, resolved without a trail.

With Dibon

Reconciliation & Observability matches every channel automatically and queues each break by its SLA, with evidence from both sides.

Channels that disagree

M-Pesa, Airtel Money, bank transfers and cards each arrive in a different place, in a different format, with a different idea of when a payment is final.

With Dibon

Kapu Pay Gateway puts every rail behind one API. A payment the rail has not confirmed is pending, never assumed.

Approvals with no record

Loans and payments approved in conversation. Nobody can show who approved what, and when.

With Dibon

Approvals and mandates route every decision by product and amount, with no self-approval, and record who decided at the moment they decided.

Customers who cannot see their money

Members phone in to ask a balance, a loan limit, or whether a contribution arrived.

With Dibon

Omni-channel banking gives every member an app, internet banking and USSD under your brand, with the same answer on each.

Growth held back by paperwork

Paper KYC, paper mandates, and monthly credit-bureau files assembled by hand.

With Dibon

Identity, e-signatures and credit-bureau automation take onboarding, consent and bureau submissions off paper.

How we build

Three principles in every product

Your money stays yours

We do not hold your money

Funds stay in your accounts, at your bank, or with a licensed partner. Our software prompts, instructs, matches and reports, so regulated custody stays where it belongs.

One source

Every record has one home

Money lives in your core. Customers and what they owe live in your ERP. Every match and every exception lives in our reconciliation engine. Nothing is quietly copied.

Evidence

Nothing is guessed

An unmatched payment stays visible until evidence places it. An unconfirmed payment is pending, not failed and not successful. Every number traces back to one transaction.
