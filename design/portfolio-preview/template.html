Who we are

One technology partner, not one product

Dibon Consulting designs and builds software for banks, Saccos, microfinance institutions, payment providers and the businesses that work with them. Payments, deposit-taking, lending, escrow and reconciliation come together with monitoring, messaging, identity and ERP integration, so an institution deals with one partner rather than five.

We work from Nairobi, and we design for the markets we serve: mobile money first, statement files where APIs do not yet exist, and feature phones alongside smartphones.

The problems we solve

Where money gets lost in the work

  • Reconciliation by handStatements downloaded from every bank and mobile-money portal, then matched line by line. Breaks found late and resolved without a trail.
  • Channels that disagreeM-Pesa, Airtel Money, bank transfers and cards each arrive in a different place, in a different format, with a different idea of when a payment is final.
  • Approvals with no recordLoans and payments approved in conversation. Nobody can show who approved what, and when.
  • Customers who cannot see their moneyMembers phone in to ask a balance, a loan limit, or whether a contribution arrived.
  • Growth held back by paperworkPaper KYC, paper mandates, and monthly credit-bureau files assembled by hand.

How we build

Three principles in every product

Your money stays yours

We do not hold your money

Funds stay in your accounts, at your bank, or with a licensed partner. Our software prompts, instructs, matches and reports, which keeps regulated custody where it belongs.

One source

Every record has one home

Money lives in your core. Customers and what they owe live in your ERP. Every match and every exception lives in our reconciliation engine. Nothing is quietly copied.

Evidence

Nothing is guessed

An unmatched payment stays visible until evidence places it. An unconfirmed payment is pending, not failed and not successful. Every number traces back to one transaction.

Kapu Collect

Deposits collected by partners, held at your bank
Specification complete

For banks and other regulated institutions reaching the large pool of collections that sits outside formal banking. Collection firms, high-volume SMEs and unlicensed funds-holding operators connect through APIs, and every deposit lands in a real account at the bank, in the end customer's name.

  • Account and sub-account opening, gated on IPRS identity checks
  • Deposits by mobile-money prompt; withdrawals to mobile, till or paybill
  • Account-to-account transfers, balances and statements
  • Partner and bank management portals
  • Asynchronous callbacks, idempotent requests, queries by reference
  • Daily reconciliation against the bank's core
Channels M-Pesa, Airtel Money, T-Kash; PesaLink to followRuns inside the bank, or in-country cloud

Kapu Pay Gateway

One API for every rail a business takes
In design

For businesses and fintechs taking payments on more than one rail. One API and one dashboard cover mobile money, bank transfers and cards, and every payment is reconciled against what it paid for. Payments settle into the merchant's own accounts.

  • One collections API across M-Pesa, Airtel Money and T-Kash
  • Cards and PesaLink
  • Payment links, including invoices sent by SMS
  • A merchant dashboard: every payment matched, every exception listed
  • Signed callbacks and idempotent requests

Kapu Escrow

Funds held at a bank until the conditions are met
In design

For property, vehicle sales, construction milestones, B2B trade and marketplaces. The buyer's money sits at a partner bank in an escrow account of its own, released to the seller only when the agreed approvers sign off. Neither party, nor Dibon, can release funds alone.

  • Digital escrow agreements, signed electronically by both parties
  • One bank-held escrow account per agreement
  • Milestones with evidence, partial releases, refunds
  • Disputes frozen and decided by a named arbiter
  • A daily tie-out of every escrow account against its agreement

Kapu MFI

Loan management for growing lenders
Specification complete

For lenders moving off spreadsheets, paper and ageing systems. The whole loan, from a digital application to closure or restructure, with the process enforced and light enough for a team of three.

  • KYC with IPRS lookup and a completeness gate per product
  • Collateral management, vehicles first, with verification evidence
  • Approval chains by product and amount, with no self-approval
  • Above-limit approvals flagged and tracked as their own cohort
  • Repayments split into principal, interest, fees and insurance
  • SMS reminders, a portfolio dashboard, and migration from Mifos
Built on Apache Fineract

Kapu Sacco

For credit-only and deposit-taking Saccos
In design

For Saccos that want contributions, deposits, loans and guarantees in one place, current every day rather than every month-end.

  • Membership, share capital and member deposits; withdrawable savings for deposit-taking Saccos
  • Contributions by M-Pesa, bank and employer check-off, matched to members
  • Loan limits from members' deposits; guarantors who can see their exposure
  • Credit-committee approval, recorded per member
  • Dividends and interest computed from your bylaws, kept for the AGM
  • Alongside your existing core, or as your core

Reconciliation & Observability

Every channel, every cycle, right now
Specification complete

Every transaction matched across the rail, the bank and your ledger. Breaks queued by how close they are to their SLA, not by size, and traced hop by hop with evidence from both sides. Suspense is the exception queue seen from the ledger: when the queue is empty, suspense is zero, and the day is closed.

  1. IngestCallbacks, APIs, MT940, CAMT, CSV
  2. MatchRules, three-way against what you expected
  3. QueueBreaks ordered by SLA, with owners
  4. ResolveEvidence first; reversals need a second person
  5. Close the dayQueue empty, suspense zero
  • A trace for every transaction, hop by hop
  • A ledger view proved two ways: real-time assertions and a nightly batch
  • Treasury, float, and branch and till cash by denomination
  • Monitoring, alerting and SLOs for everything that moves money
Built on Kubernetes, Prometheus, OpenTelemetry, Grafana

SME Reconciliation

Your bank and mobile money, inside your ERP
In design

For businesses on Odoo, with Zoho and others to follow. Bank, mobile-money and card statements flow into the ERP. Incoming money is matched to invoices, outgoing money to bills and expenses, and payments approved in the ERP are executed by bank API or mobile money. Nothing unmatched is hidden in suspense.

Omni-channel banking

App, web and USSD, under your brand
In design

A mobile app, internet banking and USSD menus, white-labelled for each institution, on one channel API, so a balance is the same everywhere. Members check balances, apply for loans, accept guarantor requests, contribute and pay. Anything that moves money asks the member to confirm who they are at that moment, after a SIM-swap check. USSD works on any phone.

The platform underneath

The same services under every product

A customer who adds a second product gets the same approvals, messages, logins and reports as the first.

Approvals and mandates

Chains by product, amount and risk; no self-approval; every decision attributed when made. Debit mandates with the payer's consent.

Messaging

SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram and app push. In Kenya, SMS goes direct to the operators under Dibon's PRSP licence.

Identity and access

One login for every system; re-authentication before money moves; financial-grade API security; IPRS and liveness checks.

Credit bureaus

Monthly submissions from the ledger, reconciled; lookups with the purpose recorded first; corrections on settlement.

Payment rails

M-Pesa, Airtel Money, T-Kash, PesaLink, bank APIs and files, and cards behind one interface. Unconfirmed means pending.

Insights

Every metric defined once, for dashboards and plain-language questions. Each answer shows how it was calculated.

Approvals, payment rails and identity are partly specified; the other services are in design.

Deployment and security

Built to be audited

  • Where it runsOn your premises, or in an in-country cloud. Kenya first; designed to follow our customers across East and Southern Africa and Nigeria.
  • How it runsCloud-native on Kubernetes, mostly on permissively licensed open source: Apache Fineract, Keycloak, Kafka, Prometheus, OpenTelemetry and Apache Superset.
  • Who can actOne login per person, never shared; re-authentication at the moment of any act that moves money; a second approver for payments, reversals and role changes.
  • What is recordedEvery action, append-only. Nothing is edited after the fact; a correction is a new entry.
  • How data is handledDesigned in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. Data is classified; the most sensitive never appears in logs or on a phone. Credentials never leave our servers.
  • How it is watchedMonitoring, alerting and service-level objectives for every system that moves money.
