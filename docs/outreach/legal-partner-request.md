Dibon's own questions (FS-432, FS-435, FS-440). Send from your own
address to the firm's banking and fintech practice; the name of the person

**Subject:** Conflict check and advice: payment service provider status,
VAT and the NPS Bill 2026

> Dear [name],
>
> Dibon Consulting builds software for banks, Saccos, microfinance
> institutions and lenders in Kenya: collections, a payments gateway,
> lending and Sacco systems, reconciliation and SMS. Our products ran in
> production at Onit Bank from February 2025 to June 2026, and since 2021

> Kenyan banks, work that continues today. We never hold customers' funds.
>
> We would like to instruct your firm on three questions, and first ask
> you to run a conflict check against Dibon Company Limited. We can share
> a list of our customers and the main competitors in our market under
> confidentiality for that purpose.
>
> 1. **Our status under the National Payment System Act.** Section 2
>    defines a payment service provider to include a person who processes
>    or stores data on behalf of one, and section 12 requires authorisation.
>    We understand the High Court in the KENEX case read that definition
>    widely. Does our gateway, which routes payments on a merchant's or an
>    institution's own shortcode and accounts without handling funds,
>    require authorisation today, and if so in which category?
> 2. **VAT under the Finance Act 2026.** From 1 July 2026, payment
>    processing, gateway and aggregation fees charged by a payment service
>    provider carry VAT. How does that apply to our fees, given question 1?
> 3. **The draft National Payment System Bill 2026.** We have drafted
>    comments, due 9 October, proposing a technology service provider
>    category for vendors that never touch funds. Could you review them
>    before we file?

> introduction, in either direction, and we would not ask for one.
