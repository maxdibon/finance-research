# Sharing the portfolio with a contact

A template for the email that sends the two named copies (FS-377). Fill
the brackets; names stay out of this file. Attach both editions built with
`--for "<name>"`, and log each copy on the CRM opportunity (FS-333).

Use it for a contact whose network is not yet known: it asks for a
conversation and introductions, and does not pitch one product.

## The email

**Subject:** Dibon's financial services portfolio, for you

> Dear [first name],
>
> [One line on how you know each other, or what you last spoke about.]
>
> As promised, I'm sharing what we have been building at Dibon. We make the
> software that Saccos, microfinance institutions, banks and businesses use
> to collect, lend, hold and reconcile money, without ever holding the money
> ourselves. Funds stay in the institution's own accounts; our software
> prompts, matches and reports.
>
> I've attached two versions, both prepared for you:
>
> - **The overview** (9 pages): who we are, the problems we solve, where an
>   institution would start, and how we deploy and secure it. A ten-minute
>   read.
> - **The full portfolio** (24 pages): every product in detail, with the
>   integrations, support and where we are going.
>
> Two things I'd value your view on:
>
> 1. **Does this match a problem you see?** Reconciliation done by hand,
>    payments that arrive in different places, approvals with no record,
>    members phoning in for a balance: if any of these sound familiar
>    among the institutions you know, I'd like to hear how they deal with it.
> 2. **Who should we be talking to?** If someone in your network runs, or
>    advises, a Sacco, MFI, bank or growing business, an introduction would
>    mean a great deal. We start with a short working session on their most
>    painful process, and then show the product running on their own data
>    before any commitment.
>
> Could we find 20 minutes in the next two weeks? [Suggest two times.]
>
> Feel free to pass the documents to anyone you think would benefit; they
> carry your name on the cover, so we'll know they came through you.
>
> With thanks,
>
> [Your name]\
> [Title], Dibon Consulting\
> [phone] · [email]
