# 3 · The calendar

Every recurring duty an institution has on the platform, by when it
falls. Each names who does it and where it comes from; times are the
institution's, on its business calendar. In the institution's Odoo,
each duty is a recurring task in Project with its owner, so a missed one
shows as overdue (FS-772); the daily duties are already tickets or
queues of their own.

## Every day

| When | Duty | Who | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| 06:00 | The morning report arrives; the morning's ticket opens | Accountant | JN-17 |
| By 09:00 | Yesterday's bank statement forwarded whole, or uploaded | Accountant | JN-17; RO-01 |
| By 10:00 | Every exception owned | Accountant | JN-17 |
| By 12:00 | The morning signed off by closing its ticket | Accountant | JN-17 |
| Through the day | Unplaced payments: answers placed; the 48-hour calls; returns at day 7 | Accountant; follow-up team | JN-16 |
| Through the day | Complaints logged the day they are made, worked to 14 days, never past 30 | Whoever hears it; complaints handler | JN-08 |
| The day it happens | A joiner, move or exit recorded in Odoo; a leaver's bank and M-Pesa access ended | Manager; finance lead | JN-18; HB-10 to HB-13 |
| 23:00 to 04:00 | Nothing released: the end-of-day window | All staff | RC-21 |
| 23:45, if told | A held close: the cause fixed, or the override with Dibon's on-call engineer | Finance lead | JN-17 (3a) |

## Every month

| When | Duty | Who | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| The last morning | Open items listed as reconciling items, each with the day it should clear | Accountant | JN-17 (3g) |
| First week | The complaints export with the regulation's fields, for the report to CBK in the form it sets | Complaints handler | JN-08; reg 40(6), (7) |
| First week | Dibon's access register for the month compared with the institution's own grants | Operations lead | SE-26 |
| Any time | Holidays for the next 30 days in the business calendar | Finance lead | End-of-day check C10 |

## Every quarter

| When | Duty | Who | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| First week | The access review, signed | Operations lead | HB-17; rules for institutions 3 |

## Every year

| When | Duty | Who | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| By 1 November | Money that could not be returned to its payer, reported to the Unclaimed Financial Assets Authority once the period has run | Finance lead | JN-16 (3f); LQ-08 |
| Twice a year | A training session with Dibon | Operations lead | Training |
| Twice a year | The fallback runbook rehearsed with Dibon, in its tabletop: names, limits and forms checked | Operations lead | JN-19; HB-70 to HB-78; business continuity |
| With each release | The handbook and manuals reviewed for the tasks that changed | Operations lead, with Dibon | Handbook rule 4 |
