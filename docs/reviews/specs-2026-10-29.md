# The specs, reviewed · 29 October 2026

**What was reviewed:** the sixteen specs other than back-office, read in
full, against the template's new "Succinct and complete" rules
([the template](../specs/_template.md), FS-1054), whose worked example
is [back-office](../specs/back-office.md): each thing said once, one
diagram, a rule as one thing that always holds, a process as one table.
Every section reference in every spec was also resolved against the
section it names. Read after FS-1053's diagram changes (86d2b6e), which
drew the lending service and the core adapter where the specs' diagrams
showed Kapu MFI; FS-1053 also reviews the specs index's order, which is
not repeated here. **Not reviewed:** whether each design is right; code,
which there is none of; or the documents the specs cite.

**The short answer:** the specs are sound, and most are close to the
standard. They have two kinds of problem. **Accuracy:** ten stale
references, two contradictions and a name that drifted. The references,
one contradiction and two small defects are corrected here (§4). **Shape:**
some things are said in two or four places, and a few specs carry a
subject that belongs elsewhere. Three specs want a restructure as
back-office had (lender-rules, lending-service, recon); seven want a
light pass; six are in shape.

## 2 · Findings

| Id | Finding | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Ten section references pointed at the wrong section.** Four references, in three specs, sent readers to "§9" of kapu-core-fineract for the checks on a running instance, which are §12 (§9 is Data); access-keycloak's "§8's checks" are §12; olp's "§9's checks" are §10; approval-engine's "a test in §11" is §14; staff-workspace's test A7 cited rule 9 for Swahili, which is rule 10; two references to back-office rules moved when back-office was restructured | Corrected (§4). Sections renumber when one is added; a check that a "§N's checks" or "tests in §N" reference lands on a checks or tests section would catch the next |
| S2 | **Two contradictions.** staff-workspace's "Out of R1" said product set-up is Dibon's, while its rule 9 and Products screen give it to the institution's product owner in R1 (FS-978). access-keycloak lists standard token exchange as "Not in R1: no service acts as a person", while staff-workspace rule 4 and §5 call the lending service and the engine as the person, by token exchange, in R1 | The first corrected, to FS-978 (§4). The second for the owner: token exchange "Yes, for the staff workspace" in access-keycloak's features, its client's settings, and a check (K15) |
| S3 | **"Kapu MFI" names two things.** Since FS-983, Kapu MFI is the product and the lending service is the service and the core's only writer (lending-service rule 2). approval-engine, kapu-core-fineract, access-keycloak (the `kapu-mfi` client), staff-workspace and github still call the service, its API and its service account "Kapu MFI" | "The lending service" for the service, its API and its account everywhere; Kapu MFI only for the product. Whether the Keycloak client is renamed `lending-service` is the owner's call |
| S4 | **One thing, several specs.** The night's order is in end-of-day-runner §3 and again in lender-rules §3; the nightly comparison of the ledger with approvals is in approval-engine §9, kapu-core-fineract §8, access-keycloak §6 and recon §8, which says recon now raises it; the collection hours are lender-rules §2.6's and messaging §3.2 restates them, with a question each for Roan Capital; observability §4's alert rules restate thresholds each spec's own §Quality sets | One owner each, the others citing it: the runner for the night (lender-rules lists only its steps 3 to 6); recon for the comparison; lender-rules for the hours; each spec for its alerts, observability listing only its own and the routes |
| S5 | **One thing said twice in a spec.** recon: what a break gets, in §4.4's table and again as §4.7's four questions; a split payment's legs in §2 and §5; who builds the companion in rule 7 and §3.1.8. lender-rules: each rule as the Regulation, then Our rule, then a Behaviour that restates Our rule before it adds the mechanism. github: the move from GitLab and its price in the opening and §2; a pull request waiting on another in §3 and §4.4. event-bus-kafka: recon's events in two rows | recon: one table of actions, with the four questions as its first column; legs in §5 only; who builds it once. lender-rules: Behaviour holds only the mechanism (FS-900's Regulation and Our rule unchanged). github and event-bus: said once |
