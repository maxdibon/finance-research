# Bank channel proposal

Every word of the two-page proposal to a bank. The build reads this file
(design/bank-channel-proposal/build.js); the layout holds no copy. Same
format as the portfolio copy. "[Bank]" is replaced by the name given to
the build; without one, the copy reads "your bank".

## Document

**Title:** Your business customers' books, reconciled by your bank
**Kicker:** A partnership proposal
**Reference:** DC-FS-PR-001

## The opportunity

**Lede:** Most of [Bank]'s business customers still reconcile their bank
and M-Pesa statements by hand, at month-end, in a spreadsheet. We propose
that [Bank] offers them Dibon's SME Recon: their statements flow from
[Bank] straight into their own books, every day.
**For your customers:**
- Every payment in and out matched to an invoice or a bill the day it
  lands, across [Bank] accounts, M-Pesa and cards
- Anything unmatched stays visible until someone places it; nothing is
  guessed
- Their own accounting system, kept: Odoo, Zoho or others; or a full Odoo
  business suite set up for them
**For the bank:**
- A business account wired into the customer's books is an account that
  stays
- With the customer's consent, reconciled cash flow to lend against,
  rather than a statement to interpret
- Something to compete on besides rates, with no build on your side
**How it works:**
- You introduce: In your app, your branches and through your relationship
  managers
- We connect: Your statement feed, by API or daily file, into the
  business's own books
- They reconcile: Every day, with Dibon's support from the first login

## Partnership

**Heading:** Two ways to partner
**Intro:** [Bank] never resells software. It introduces its customers to
us, or includes SME Recon in a business package of its own.
**Referral:**
- Who contracts with the business: Dibon
- Who pays Dibon: the business
- What the bank earns: a referral fee, where the bank may take one
- Branding: Dibon's, presented by [Bank]
**Bundle:**
- Who contracts with the business: [Bank], under its account terms
- Who pays Dibon: [Bank], per active business
- What the bank earns: whatever its package earns
- Branding: [Bank]'s package, with SME Recon named inside it
**Recommendation:** Start with a referral pilot; move to a bundle once
take-up is shown.
**Data heading:** Data and consent
**Data:**
- The business owns its books. [Bank] sees who enrolled and whether they
  are active, nothing more
- Cash-flow summaries reach [Bank] only with the business's separate,
  recorded and withdrawable consent, for a stated purpose
- We never use [Bank]'s or its customers' data for our own purposes
- One consent screen, supplied by us, so every customer is asked the same
  way
**Pilot heading:** A pilot
**Pilot:**
- A cohort of [Bank]'s business customers and a period, agreed with you
- Measured by businesses enrolled, active after 90 days, the share of
  statement lines matched without a person, and support load
- For [Bank]: accounts retained, and lending conversations started with
  consent
- Then together: widen the referral, move to a bundle, or stop
**From the bank:**
- A statement feed for each business that opts in: API preferred, or daily
  MT940, CAMT.053 or CSV files
- A product owner, and a way to reach your business customers
- Agreement on the consent screen
**Why Dibon:**
- We ran collections and payments in production at Onit Bank, with
  W-Harambee, from 2025 to 2026
- We supply SMS and messaging to two tier-1 Kenyan banks today, and built
  USSD and digital-lending channels for one of them from 2022
- An Odoo partner and a Zoho partner; we implement the business suite
  ourselves
- Built and hosted in Kenya; we never hold your customers' money
