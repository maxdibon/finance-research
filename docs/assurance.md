# Assurance services — testing for institutions we do not supply

**What we offer:** penetration tests, IT and cyber assessments, and
independent testing of an institution's AML/CFT system, for banks,
microfinance banks, Saccos, lenders and payment service providers
(FS-438, FS-440). Each is a fixed-scope engagement with a written report
to the institution's board or audit committee.

**Why it is open to us:** CBK's cybersecurity guidance note (2017) and its
guideline for payment service providers (2019) require independent
testing and allow it to be outsourced; the POCAML Regulations 2023 require
an independent audit function to test an institution's AML system (regs
11(d) and 43; law register LR-14), and nothing requires it to be internal
or licensed. No licence for penetration testers was found (FS-438).

**What it is not:** a statutory financial audit, which needs an
ICPAK-licensed firm approved by CBK or SASRA; an ISO certification, which
needs a KENAS-accredited body; a data protection compliance audit, until
ODPC accredits us (docs/compliance.md LC-14); or an audit of critical
information infrastructure, which only the national coordination
committee's appointed auditors may do. We refer those to licensed firms.

## Rules

1. **Never for a customer.** We do not test, assess or audit any
   institution we supply, host or operate a system for, or whose
   compliance operations we run (financial-crime FC-15). Independence is
   the product; one exception spends it.
2. **Every engagement is checked first** against docs/customers.md and
   recorded below before work starts. The check tool fails if an
   engagement names a customer.
3. **Written authorisation before any test.** Unauthorised access to a
   computer system is an offence (Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act
   s.14; law register LR-20). The authorisation names the systems, the
   dates and who may stop the test.
4. **Qualified people.** Testers hold a recognised certification; an AML
   test is led by someone with an AML qualification. Until we have them,
   we do not sell the service.
5. **The report is the institution's.** We keep working papers for seven
   years, and nothing from an engagement is used in a sale.
