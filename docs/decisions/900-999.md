## FS-900 · A rule is ours, and cites the law it applies

*Entry written 2026-10-21 by Claude (Assistant), from the owner's words in chat.*

- **Chose:** "rule" means our application or reading of the law; each of
  our rules says so and cites the provision it applies, naming the legal
  question where the reading is unsettled. Each lender rule carries an
  "Our rule" line between what the law requires and what the platform
  does. A law's own provisions are cited as the law names them: "s." for
  an Act's sections, "reg" for regulations, "r." for a set of Rules; and
  spelled out (section, regulation, rule) for readers outside Dibon.
- **Requires:** docs/specs/lender-rules.md "Our rule"; docs/law/README.md "alone is ours: our".
- **Argued (owner):** "rule, as it's our application/understanding of
  the regs, and then we cite the actual reg"; and the short forms spelled
  out for external documents.
- **Argued against:** citing the Auctioneers Rules as "rule 12", which
  would blur our rules with the law's; leaving our readings inside the
  behaviour, where counsel cannot tell them from the law.
- **Costs (inferred):** every new lender rule written in three parts;
  "r." added to the citation tool, upper-case "R." left to other
  frameworks (a FATF Recommendation).

  left nothing approvable while he was away (FS-898).
- **Costs (inferred):** each person to confirm their row and handle;
  rows handed out from the CTIO's 141 to the leads, on his approval.

## FS-903 · Our people open to the team; a customer's people by role, named only to its project

  customer's roles are written here, by role only; its people's names and

## FS-932 · OpenTofu for infrastructure as code

*Entry written 2026-10-22 by Claude for Eric (Assistant), from the owner's words in chat.*

- **Chose:** OpenTofu, with cloud-init on the machines, for every piece
  of infrastructure: first the sandbox on public Azure for the MVP day,
  then the MVP's network and cluster, then production on Azure Stack.
  Its state lives in Azure storage, signed into with Entra and
  versioned; never in git. What runs inside the clusters stays Flux's.
- **Requires:** docs/architecture/technology.md "OpenTofu, with cloud-init".
- **Argued (owner):** "go ahead with opentofu and the setup", after asking
  whether the script would be Terraform.
- **Argued against:** Terraform, now under the Business Source License,
  as Vault is, which we declined for OpenBao; Bicep, which speaks only
  Azure; Pulumi, heavier to learn for a team starting in an afternoon; a
  plain command-line script, which leaves nothing to tear down by.
- **Costs (inferred):** a state store to keep and guard; OpenTofu on each
  machine that runs it; HashiCorp's documentation read with care where
  the two tools have drifted apart.

## FS-970 · The back office behind one interface, so the ERP can change

*Entry written 2026-10-26 by Claude for Eric (Assistant), from the owner's words in chat.*

- **Chose:** design for leaving Odoo, or for a second ERP, from R1: our
  services reach the back office only through a back-office gateway,
  one adapter per ERP, one contract test suite; no business rule in the
  ERP; our references as the keys; signed documents in the vault too;
  everything exportable. Odoo Enterprise stays the back office for now
  (FS-644); Odoo Community with OCA (FS-967), ERPNext and Tryton (FS-969)
  are candidates to evaluate.
- **Requires:** BS-21, BS-22; docs/specs/back-office.md.
- **Argued (owner):** "my primary thought is how to remain flexible and
  ensure that if we need to port from odoo, or support another platform
  for the 'erp inside our platform'. let's design for this."
- **Argued against:** naming Odoo in every service's integration, the
  cheapest today and the dearest to leave; choosing a second ERP now,
  before a customer asks for one; Flectra and ERP5 (FS-969).
- **Costs (inferred):** one more service in R1, small; Odoo's features
  used through our words, so a feature without a job waits for one; a
  contract test suite kept for every adapter.
