# Customers: delivery maps

A delivery map takes one customer's own requirements, row by row, and
sets each against the capabilities that meet it, with how well they fit
and what is still open. The register of every customer, past and
present, is [customers.md](../customers.md); a map exists here only for
a customer we are delivering to.

| Customer | Stage | Delivery map |
|---|---|---|
| Roan Capital (CU-01) | In delivery: R1, the pilot | [roan-capital.md](roan-capital.md): 43 requirements against capabilities, the out-of-scope list, the open answers |

**What each map holds:**

1. **The engagement:** who, what, the document it answers.
2. **Requirements against capabilities:** each requirement with its
   capabilities and its fit: *Covered*, or *Partial* with the gap stated.
3. **Out of scope,** as the customer's document says.
4. **Open answers:** the questions only the customer can settle.
5. **Where the detail is.**

**To add one:** when a customer reaches "In delivery" in
[customers.md](../customers.md), copy the shape of an existing map,
link it from the customer's row, and keep its capabilities and the row's
"Depends on" the same: the check holds the two together.
