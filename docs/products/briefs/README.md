# Product briefs

FS-1029. Internal write-ups of products that are difficult or costly for
an institution to put together on its own, and what we bring to each.
Each says what the product is and why it is interesting, how it works,
in a diagram, what makes it hard, what we bring (with the capability
ids), what we have run or learnt before, what is still open, and who
might want it. Internal first; a version for prospects may follow.
