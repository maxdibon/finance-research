# Banking Act (Cap. 488)

The law register's row is [LR-04](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Banking Act (Cap. 488) |
| Regulator | Central Bank of Kenya |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | How banks and other institutions under it are licensed and supervised by the Central Bank, and limits on what they may do, among them the cap on what may be recovered on a defaulted loan |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 12 | An institution may not trade, wholesale or retail, import or export, except in recovering debts owed to it (12(a)), and is restricted in the shares and property it may hold |
| s. 44A | What an institution may recover on a non-performing loan is capped at the principal then owing, interest up to that principal, and recovery expenses; the cap is measured from the last time the loan became non-performing; interest under a court order after the order is outside it. The same cap as reg 29 of the Credit Providers Regulations, for banks |
