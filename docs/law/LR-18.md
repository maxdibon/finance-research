# Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021

The law register's row is [LR-18](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, Legal Notice No. 263 of 2021 |
| Regulator | Office of the Data Protection Commissioner |
| Made under | The Data Protection Act (Cap. 411C) |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | How the Data Protection Act's rights and duties work in practice: requests from data subjects, their timings and forms, and the duties of those who hold personal data |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| reg 9 | A data subject may ask whether their data is processed and see it, with its purposes, recipients, retention and source; access given within seven days, in a common electronic form where asked electronically |
| reg 10 | Untrue, inaccurate, outdated, incomplete or misleading data corrected within fourteen days, free of charge; a refusal given in writing, with reasons, within seven days |
| reg 11 | A data subject's data moved, on request, to another holder of their choosing within thirty days, for a reasonable fee no more than the cost; a refusal given in writing, with reasons, within seven days |
