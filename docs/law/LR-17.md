# Data Protection Act (Cap. 411C)

The law register's row is [LR-17](../regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Data Protection Act (Cap. 411C) |
| Regulator | Office of the Data Protection Commissioner |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | The rights of people whose personal data is processed, and the duties of those who hold it: a lawful basis, limits on use and retention, impact assessments, breach notice, sensitive data, and transfers out of Kenya |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 26 | A data subject's rights: to be told how their data is used, to see it, to object to its processing, and to have false or misleading data corrected or deleted |
| s. 30 | No processing without a lawful basis: the person's consent for a stated purpose, or necessity for a contract, a legal obligation, vital interests, a public task or another ground the section lists |
| s. 31 | An impact assessment before processing likely to put people's rights at high risk, describing the processing, its necessity, the risks and the safeguards |
| s. 32 | Whoever relies on consent proves it was given for the purpose; consent can be withdrawn at any time; it is not freely given where a service is made to depend on consent to processing it does not need |
| s. 39 | Personal data kept only as long as needed for its purpose, unless the law, a lawful purpose or the person's consent requires longer; then deleted, erased, anonymised or pseudonymised |
| s. 43 | A breach with a real risk of harm notified to the Data Commissioner within seventy-two hours, and to the person affected; a processor tells the controller within forty-eight hours where practicable (43(3)) |
| s. 48 | Personal data transferred out of Kenya only with proof of appropriate safeguards given to the Data Commissioner, or where the transfer is necessary on a ground the section lists |
