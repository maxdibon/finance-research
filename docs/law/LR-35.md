# Companies (Beneficial Ownership Information) Regulations

The law register's row is [LR-35](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Companies (Beneficial Ownership Information) Regulations, Legal Notice No. 12 of 2020 |
| Regulator | The Registrar of Companies |
| Also called | Beneficial Ownership Information Regulations · BO Regulations |
| Made under | The Companies Act (Cap. 486) |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | Who counts as a company's beneficial owner, and how the company keeps and files their details, and who may see them |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| reg 3 | Every company has a beneficial owner: a natural person who, alone or jointly, directly or indirectly, holds at least ten per cent of the shares or votes, can appoint or remove most of the board, or exercises significant influence or control (3(2)) |
| reg 14 | The Registrar may disclose beneficial ownership information to authorities and regulators; and, on Form BOF6 and the fee, to financial institutions, without protected personal information (14(1)(c)) |
