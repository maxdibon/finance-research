# Advocates Act (Cap. 16)

The law register's row is [LR-40](../regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Advocates Act (Cap. 16) |
| Regulator | The Law Society of Kenya and the Advocates Disciplinary Tribunal; offences go to the courts |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | Who may practise as an advocate in Kenya, and the conduct required of advocates and of those who deal with them |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 37 | An advocate who agrees to share the profits of professional business with anyone who is not an advocate commits an offence; a bonus to the advocate's own staff is not caught |
| s. 38 | Whoever, not being qualified, procures or tries to procure an advocate's employment for payment or any advantage, or asks an advocate for one, is a tout; the Chief Justice may bar a tout from employment by advocates |
