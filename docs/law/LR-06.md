# Banking (Credit Reference Bureau) Regulations, 2020

The law register's row is [LR-06](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Banking (Credit Reference Bureau) Regulations, 2020, Legal Notice No. 55 of 2020 |
| Regulator | Central Bank of Kenya |
| Also called | CRB Regulations · CRB regulations · CRB Regulations 2020 |
| Made under | The Banking Act (Cap. 488), with the Microfinance Act and the Central Bank of Kenya Act |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | How credit reference bureaus are licensed, and how institutions share, use and correct customers' credit information through them |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| reg 2 | Its definitions; a "subscriber" is an institution licensed under the Banking Act, the Microfinance Act or the Sacco Societies Act that has subscribed to receive customer information from a bureau |
| reg 67 | A notice to a customer served by registered post, email, text message to the registered number, or delivery against acknowledgement; a record of every notice served kept for at least seven years (67(2)) |
