# Employment Act (Cap. 226)

The law register's row is [LR-30](../regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Employment Act (Cap. 226) |
| Regulator | The Ministry responsible for labour; disputes go to the Employment and Labour Relations Court |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | The minimum terms of employment: contracts and written particulars, wages and deductions, leave, termination and records |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 10 | Written particulars of employment given to each employee, and kept for five years after the employment ends (10(6)) |
| s. 19 | Deductions from wages only those the section allows, among them one the employee asks for in writing (19(1)(g)); all deductions together no more than two-thirds of wages (19(3)); a deduction paid over as the law or agreement requires, failing which an offence (19(5)) |
| s. 79 | A register of employees, with each one's name, age, sex, occupation, start date, nationality and education, and an annual return of employees by 31 January |
