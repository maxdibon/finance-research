# National Payment System Regulations, 2014

The law register's row is [LR-12](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The National Payment System Regulations, 2014, Legal Notice No. 109 of 2014 |
| Regulator | Central Bank of Kenya |
| Also called | NPS Regulations · NPS Regulations 2014 |
| Made under | The National Payment System Act (Cap. 491A) |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | How payment service providers are authorised and run: applications, agents, e-money, consumer protection and records |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| reg 4 | A person, other than a bank or other institution, applies to the Central Bank for authorisation before acting as a payment service provider, on Form 1, with its registration documents, the services and programme of operations, the public interest served and a business plan |
| reg 29 | A payment service provider keeps an accurate, fully accessible audit trail of every transaction, and records of every electronic transfer for at least seven years (29(1)(b)); monthly returns to the Central Bank |
