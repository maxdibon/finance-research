# Central Bank of Kenya (Non-Deposit Taking Credit Providers) Regulations, 2026

The law register's row is [LR-03](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Central Bank of Kenya (Non-Deposit Taking Credit Providers) Regulations, 2026, Legal Notice No. 191 of 2026 |
| Regulator | Central Bank of Kenya |
| Also called | CBK lender regulations · lender regulations · Credit Providers Regulations · CBK Non-Deposit Taking Credit Providers Regulations · Lenders' regulations · NDTCP Regulations · NDTCP |
| Made under | The Central Bank of Kenya Act (Cap. 491), sections 33R, 57(3)(a), (aa) and 57(4); made by the Central Bank of Kenya on 4 September 2026 |
| Published | Kenya Gazette Supplement No. 234, 29 September 2026 |
| In force | Yes. A lender already in business applies for a licence or registration within six months of publication (reg 96) |
| What it is | How the Central Bank licenses, registers and supervises lenders that take no deposits, and the conduct it requires of them: credit policy, appraisal, collection, disclosure, pricing, complaints and data |

## Provisions we cite

What each provision requires, in plain words; read from the text the law
register names. A cite of a part (reg 28(1)(b)) is covered by the row of
its whole (reg 28).

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| reg 6 | A licence, for a lender with initial capital of at least twenty million shillings, applied for in Form 1 with: its incorporation documents and registered address; its certificate of registration with the Data Commissioner; statements on complying with Part VII of the Consumer Protection Act and Part VI of the Competition Act; evidence and source of capital; its ICT system and delivery channels described; its products and their terms; the draft agreement with a delivery-channel provider; its consumer protection measures and complaints redress; its operations (loan sizes, interest rates, classification, the non-performing period); its funding and debt-to-equity ratio; its credit policy, code of conduct, consumer protection, anti-money laundering, data protection, ICT security and corporate governance policies; a sworn declaration on the source of funds; the fees; fit and proper forms for directors, the CEO, senior officers and significant shareholders, with certificates of good conduct, tax compliance certificates and credit reports; and its pricing parameters (6(2)(a) to (dd)) |
| reg 9 | Registration, for a lender that does not meet the conditions for a licence, applied for in Form 2 with much the same as reg 6, the policies as briefs, and its business continuity plans (9(3)(v)) |
| reg 22 | The Central Bank told at least thirty days before any agreement or arrangement with a third party to invest in the lender or finance its activities (22(4)); ten percent or more of its shares transferred only with the Central Bank's prior approval (22(5)) |
| reg 25 | A written credit policy, consistent with the law, stating among other things the cooling-off period (25(2)(f)) and how the borrower's ability to repay is determined (25(2)(s)) |
| reg 27 | Thirty days' notice to the Central Bank before a new delivery channel rolls out, with the mobile money account, apps and bank accounts to be used; a way to opt out of marketing messages; channels that are available, safe, protected from fraud, easy to use and affordable |
| reg 28 | Applications considered under the credit policy, ability to repay assessed and no reckless lending (28(1)); a refusal given at once, with reasons (28(2)); a loan may be restructured on the borrower's request or with prior notice (28(3)) |
| reg 29 | What can be recovered on a non-performing loan is capped at the principal then owing, interest up to that principal, and reasonable recovery costs; the cap is measured from the last time the loan became non-performing (29(3)); interest under a court order after the order is outside it (29(4)) |
| reg 30 | No harassing, shaming or threatening collection: nothing posted to shame a borrower (30(1)(d)), no messages to their contacts (30(1)(e)), no calls outside working hours, no taking of essential personal effects (30(1)(k)); no charges not disclosed in the contract (30(2)); land security recovered under the Land Act (30(3)); thirty days' notice before selling movable security (30(4)) |
| reg 31 | Material services may be outsourced, but never the final decision to lend, management and control, the board's decisions, determining compliance with the law (31(1)(d)), or managing the loan book; the lender stays liable for its provider; the Central Bank told thirty days before, with the provider's background, owners and directors (31(3)); the contract gives the Central Bank oversight of, and access to, the provider's premises, books, records, systems and staff (31(5)) |
| reg 33 | Customers' credit information shared with licensed credit reference bureaus, timely, complete and accurate; no negative listing for an amount of one thousand shillings or less (33(3)) |
| reg 37 | Branches, websites, apps and digital platforms accessible to people with physical, visual, auditory and cognitive impairments, and staff trained to serve them |
| reg 38 | Before contracting, the customer is told they are free not to enter a contract that seems onerous or unsuitable, and of any difficulty their financial position may cause in performing it |
| reg 39 | A receipt for every transaction, a free loan statement on request and a way to see it at any time (39(1)); errors corrected promptly (39(2)); a guarantor kept informed through periodic statements (39(3)) |
| reg 40 | A complaints mechanism made known to customers; acknowledgement within seven days where a complaint cannot be dealt with promptly; an oral complaint unresolved after forty-eight hours confirmed in writing; resolution within thirty days |
| reg 41 | No unfair, deceptive, oppressive or aggressive practices, among them threatening, intimidating, abusing, ignoring or humiliating a customer (41(1)(a)); no bribes (41(1)(b)); no recruiting other borrowers to earn a benefit (41(1)(c)); no discrimination (41(1)(d)); no misleading or misadvising (41(1)(e)); no reckless or negligent lending (41(1)(f)); no product a customer must buy to get the one they want (41(2)) |
| reg 42 | A guarantor's consent sought and obtained before the appointment; recourse to a guarantor only after non-judicial recovery from the borrower is exhausted, and after fourteen days' notice of the default |
| reg 43 | Recovery expenses only those absolutely necessary, each itemised and justified to the borrower; a sale of a customer's or guarantor's property done transparently |
| reg 45 | Customers' personal and contact records updated from time to time, and at least every two years (45(a)); reliable services, channels and customer-facing staff |
| reg 47 | Product information fair, clear and current, and easily available at branches, websites and every channel (47(1)(d)); the lender says it is regulated by the Central Bank, and shows its company name |
| reg 48 | A key information document for each product: its benefits, risks, terms and conditions, in summary |
| reg 49 | A business or trade name used in place of the company name is accompanied, conspicuously, by the company name |
| reg 54 | At signing, the customer receives the agreement, the repayment schedule and the total cost of credit; the agreement states the loan, charges, rates, the APR, early repayment without charge (54(4)(h)) and more (54(4)); a variation needs thirty days' notice and the borrower's acceptance (54(7)) |
| reg 55 | Pricing parameters and credit terms change only with the Central Bank's prior written approval, after thirty days' notice to the customer and the customer's acceptance |
| reg 56 | A repayment credited on the day it is paid, applied to due principal, then fees and charges, then interest; early repayment, in whole or part, without notice or penalty (56(3)), and no interest for the remaining term on full early repayment (56(4)) |
| reg 57 | A loan is non-performing when principal or interest is outstanding thirty days past due, for a loan of thirty days or less, or ninety days, for a longer one (57(1)); the credit policy (57(2)) and each loan agreement (57(3)) state the default period |
| reg 58 | Personal data processed lawfully, fairly and transparently; consent express, specific, separately obtained on its own form, withdrawable and recorded; a privacy notice is not consent |
| reg 59 | A pricing model with clear parameters: its components and costs justified, rates that may vary with the borrower's creditworthiness (59(2)(c)), the criteria for non-performance, credit bureau scores, all-inclusive pricing except third-party costs (59(2)(f)), and the APR disclosed |
| reg 60 | Where artificial intelligence makes lending decisions: decisions explained, customers told they are dealing with a machine, human oversight kept, bias assessed, models robust and explainable, data protected |
| reg 63 | Customer due diligence, identity verified from independent source documents; for a transaction not face to face, the identity document is that of the person actually transacting (63(2)); a guard against impersonation (63(3)) |
| reg 64 | Compliance with the Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act and the Prevention of Terrorism Act, on pain of the Central Bank's penalties |
| reg 96 | A lender in business when the Regulations began applies within six months of publication and may continue meanwhile; one licensed under the Digital Credit Providers Regulations, 2022 is deemed licensed |
