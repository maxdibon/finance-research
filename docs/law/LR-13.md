# Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act (Cap. 59A)

The law register's row is [LR-13](../regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act (Cap. 59A) |
| Regulator | The Financial Reporting Centre, with each institution's supervisor |
| Also called | POCAMLA · POCAML Act |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | The offences of money laundering, and the duties of reporting institutions: knowing the customer, reporting suspicious transactions, and keeping records |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 8 | Tipping off is an offence: telling anyone that a suspicious transaction report is being prepared or has been made |
| s. 44 | Complex, unusual, suspicious or large transactions monitored and suspicions reported to the Centre; the findings kept for at least seven years and made available to the Centre, the supervisor and auditors (44(5)); cash transactions above the prescribed amount reported in all cases |
| s. 46 | A reporting institution keeps records of every transaction and of the evidence of each customer's identity, in the account holder's correct name, for at least seven years from the transaction or the end of the relationship, or longer where the Centre asks (46(4)) |
