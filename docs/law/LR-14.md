# Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Regulations, 2023

The law register's row is [LR-14](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Regulations, 2023, Legal Notice No. 153 of 2023 |
| Regulator | The Financial Reporting Centre, with each institution's supervisor |
| Also called | POCAML Regs · POCAML Regulations · POCAML Regulations 2023 · AML regulations |
| Made under | The Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act (Cap. 59A) |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | How reporting institutions carry out the Act's duties: risk assessment, customer due diligence for people and companies, wire transfers, records, audit and reporting |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| reg 2 | Its definitions; "official documents" are the originals or certified copies of documents, or the official records the Act lists |
| reg 11 | Internal controls against money laundering, terrorism financing and proliferation financing, sized to the business: compliance arrangements with a reporting officer, staff screening and training, an independent audit function to test the system (11(d)), and risk assessment |
| reg 12 | A Money Laundering Reporting Officer of management level, competent, with authority and independence, whose appointment or removal is notified to the Centre and the supervisor within fourteen days; not the internal auditor or the chief executive |
| reg 21 | Simplified customer due diligence only where lower risk has been identified by an adequate risk analysis, and never where money laundering or financing of terrorism is suspected, or in a higher-risk scenario |
| reg 22 | The natural persons behind a company or other legal arrangement identified and verified, with its business, ownership and control understood, from its registration details, directors, shareholders and senior managers |
| reg 23 | Someone acting for a customer is verified as authorised, and identified and verified themselves |
| reg 26 | For politically exposed persons: a system to find them, senior management approval for the relationship, the source of wealth and funds established, and enhanced ongoing monitoring |
| reg 32 | Every wire transfer carries the originator's name, account or reference and address or ID, and the beneficiary's name and account or reference; intermediary and receiving institutions have risk-based rules for a transfer lacking them, to execute, reject or suspend it (32(6)); no transfer to or from a person designated under the UN Security Council's terrorism resolutions (32(8)) |
| reg 33 | Elements of due diligence may be relied on from a third party, under a written agreement, but the institution stays responsible for them |
| reg 42 | Records of every transaction kept for at least seven years from its completion or the end of the relationship (42(1)); and the due-diligence records, copies of official documents, account files and correspondence, for the same period (42(2)) |
| reg 43 | An independent audit function checks the institution's compliance with the Act and these Regulations |
| reg 44 | An annual compliance report to the Centre by 31 January, on the Act, the Regulations and the institution's own rules |
