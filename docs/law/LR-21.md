# Kenya Information and Communications Act (Cap. 411A)

The law register's row is [LR-21](../regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Kenya Information and Communications Act (Cap. 411A) |
| Regulator | Communications Authority of Kenya |
| Also called | KICA |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | Communications licensing and, in its part on electronic transactions, the legal standing of electronic records and signatures |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 83B | The part on electronic transactions does not apply to the creation or execution of a will, or to negotiable instruments; the Cabinet Secretary may add or remove classes by order. Everything else may be done electronically under that part |
| s. 83H | Where the law requires records kept for a period, keeping them electronically satisfies it, if they stay accessible, are kept in their original format or one shown to be accurate, and show their origin, destination and dates |
