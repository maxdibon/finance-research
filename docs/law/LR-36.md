# Public Holidays Act (Cap. 110)

The law register's row is [LR-36](../regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Public Holidays Act (Cap. 110) |
| Regulator | The Cabinet Secretary, who may declare further holidays |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | Which days are public holidays, what happens when one falls on a Sunday, and how others are declared |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 2 | The public holidays in the Schedule, with Islamic and Hindu holidays for those faiths; and the day of a general election (2(4)) and the day a President-elect is sworn in (2(5)) |
| s. 3 | The Cabinet Secretary may declare a further or substitute public holiday by notice in the Gazette, for all of Kenya or part of it |
| s. 4 | A public holiday in Part I of the Schedule falling on a Sunday moves to the next day that is not a holiday |
