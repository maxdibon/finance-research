# Auctioneers Act (Cap. 526)

The law register's row is [LR-41](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Auctioneers Act (Cap. 526) |
| Regulator | The Auctioneers Licensing Board, which licenses auctioneers and hears complaints against them |
| Also called | Auctioneers Act |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | Who may act as an auctioneer, and how auctioneers repossess, attach, store and sell property, under rules the Act provides for (the Auctioneers Rules) |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 9 | No one carries on business as an auctioneer in Kenya without a licence from the Board (9(1)); doing so is an offence (9(2)) |
| s. 21 | A sale by auction is advertised in the prescribed manner, and held on the date, at the time and place advertised (21(1)); whether it has a reserve price is stated (21(3)) |
| s. 22 | Unless agreed otherwise, the auctioneer pays the seller the net proceeds within fifteen days of the sale (22(2)) |
| s. 23 | An auctioneer acts as an officer of the court (23(a)) and under the prescribed rules when repossessing, attaching, storing or selling property under any law or contract (23(b)) |
