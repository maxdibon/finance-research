# Unclaimed Financial Assets Act (Cap. 494)

The law register's row is [LR-38](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Unclaimed Financial Assets Act (Cap. 494; No. 40 of 2011) |
| Regulator | The Unclaimed Financial Assets Authority |
| Also called | UFAA |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | When money and other financial assets count as abandoned, and how holders report them and pay them over to the Authority, which keeps them for their owners |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 13 | Assets held in a fiduciary capacity are presumed abandoned if the owner has shown no interest in them for two years after they became payable |
| s. 14 | A gift certificate, gift card or credit memo is presumed abandoned after five years unclaimed or unused |
| s. 19 | A holder makes all reasonable efforts to find and notify the owner, and notifies them between sixty days and a year before reporting the assets |
| s. 20 | A certified report of assets presumed abandoned, to the Authority, by 1 November each year for the year to 30 June |
| s. 22 | The assets reported paid or delivered to the Authority when the report is filed |
| s. 23 | The Authority takes custody; a holder that pays over in good faith is relieved of liability for the assets |
| s. 32 | A holder keeps the owner's name and last known address for ten years after the assets became reportable |
