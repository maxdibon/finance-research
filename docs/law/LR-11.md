# National Payment System Act (Cap. 491A)

The law register's row is [LR-11](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The National Payment System Act (Cap. 491A) |
| Regulator | Central Bank of Kenya |
| Also called | NPS Act |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | How payment systems and payment service providers are designated, authorised and overseen by the Central Bank |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 2 | Its definitions; a "payment service provider" includes whoever provides services for sending, receiving, storing or processing payments electronically, and any person that processes or stores data on behalf of such a provider or its users |
| s. 26 | The Central Bank, settlement system participants and the operators of payment systems keep every record from operating a payment system or issuing a payment instrument for seven years from the record's date; electronic records suffice |
