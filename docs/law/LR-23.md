# Movable Property Security Rights Act (Cap. 499A)

The law register's row is [LR-23](../regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Movable Property Security Rights Act (Cap. 499A) |
| Regulator | The Registrar of security rights, who keeps the collateral registry |
| Also called | MPSR Act |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | How security over movable property (vehicles, equipment, stock, receivables) is created, registered, given priority and enforced |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 30 | A registered notice is effective for the period the registrant states, at most ten years; it can be extended only within the six months before it expires, by an amendment notice |
| s. 31 | The Registrar sends the registrant a copy of each registered notice; the registrant sends it to the grantor within ten working days (31(2)), and failing to is an offence (31(3)) |
| s. 33 | The secured creditor registers an amendment or cancellation notice where a notice exceeds what the grantor authorised, collateral is released, or the security right is extinguished; free of charge, and within ten working days of the grantor's request, or the grantor may go to the Registrar |
| s. 65 | After the debtor fails to pay or perform, the secured creditor may use the rights in this Part, the security agreement or any other law (65(1)); the grantor's rights in this Part cannot be waived or varied before default (65(3)); under a hire-purchase agreement, only as the Hire-Purchase Act allows (65(4)) |
| s. 67 | On default, a written notice to the grantor to pay or perform, stating the default, the amount and the time by which to pay, what follows if not, and the right to apply to court (67(1), (2)); if not met in that time, the secured creditor may sue, appoint a receiver, lease, take possession or sell (67(3)) |
| s. 69 | Anyone affected may redeem the collateral by paying the secured obligation in full with the reasonable cost of enforcement, until it is sold or otherwise disposed of |
| s. 71 | Possession without a court where the security agreement consents to it, or where whoever holds the collateral does not object when it is taken (71(1)); the collateral may be made unusable and sold where it is (71(2)) |
| s. 72 | After default, the secured creditor may sell, lease or license the collateral as it is or after commercially reasonable preparation, choosing the method, time and place (72(1), (2)) |
| s. 73 | A notice of the intended disposition to the grantor, the debtor and any other secured creditor with a registered notice (73(1)), at least five working days before the sale, describing the collateral, the amount owing with an estimate of enforcement costs, the manner and the date (73(2)); not needed where the collateral may perish within ten working days (73(5)) |
| s. 74 | Proceeds go first to the reasonable cost of repossessing, holding, preparing and selling, then to the secured obligation, then to a subordinate right that has asked (74(1)); the debtor remains liable for any shortfall (74(4)) |
