# Movable Property Security Rights Act (Cap. 499A)

The law register's row is [LR-23](../regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Movable Property Security Rights Act (Cap. 499A) |
| Regulator | The Registrar of security rights, who keeps the collateral registry |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | How security over movable property (vehicles, equipment, stock, receivables) is created, registered, given priority and enforced |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 30 | A registered notice is effective for the period the registrant states, at most ten years; it can be extended only within the six months before it expires, by an amendment notice |
| s. 31 | The Registrar sends the registrant a copy of each registered notice; the registrant sends it to the grantor within ten working days (31(2)), and failing to is an offence (31(3)) |
| s. 33 | The secured creditor registers an amendment or cancellation notice where a notice exceeds what the grantor authorised, collateral is released, or the security right is extinguished; free of charge, and within ten working days of the grantor's request, or the grantor may go to the Registrar |
