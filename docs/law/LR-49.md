# Competition Act (Cap. 504)

The law register's row is [LR-49](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Competition Act (Cap. 504), formerly No. 12 of 2010 |
| Regulator | The Competition Authority of Kenya |
| Also called | Competition Act |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | Competition and consumer welfare; Part VI protects consumers from false representations and unconscionable conduct, and a lender's licence application states how it complies with it (LR-03 reg 6(2)(g)) |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 55 | No false or misleading representation in supplying or promoting goods or services: about their standard, price, terms or any right or remedy |
| s. 56 | No conduct that is, in all the circumstances, unconscionable, in supplying goods or services to a consumer |
| s. 57 | The same in business transactions, supplying to or acquiring from another business |
| s. 70 | A breach of Part VI an offence: imprisonment of up to five years, a fine of up to ten million shillings, or both |
| s. 70A | The Authority may investigate a consumer complaint on its own initiative or on information from anyone |
