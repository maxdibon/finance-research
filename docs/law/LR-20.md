# Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act (Cap. 79C)

The law register's row is [LR-20](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act (Cap. 79C) |
| Regulator | The National Computer and Cybercrimes Co-ordination Committee; offences go to the courts |
| Also called | CMCA |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | The offences of misusing computers and data, such as unauthorised access and interference, and how they are investigated |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 14 | Unauthorised access to a computer system, by infringing its security measures, is an offence: a fine of up to five million shillings, three years' imprisonment, or both |
