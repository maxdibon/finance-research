# Co-operative Societies Act (Cap. 490)

The law register's row is [LR-26](../regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Co-operative Societies Act (Cap. 490) |
| Regulator | The Commissioner for Co-operative Development |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | How co-operative societies, Saccos among them, are registered, governed and supervised, and the rights and duties of their members |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 35 | An employer that deducts a member's contribution for a co-operative society must remit it within seven days, or pay it with compound interest of at least five per cent a month; the Commissioner may sue for it |
