# Limitation of Actions Act (Cap. 22)

The law register's row is [LR-32](../regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Limitation of Actions Act (Cap. 22) |
| Regulator | None: the courts apply it |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | The time within which a claim may be brought in court, by kind of claim, and when that time starts or starts again |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 4 | Claims in contract or tort brought within six years of the cause of action; a claim on a judgment within twelve years of it (4(4)) |
| s. 19 | A claim for a principal sum secured by a mortgage of land or movable property, or for the proceeds of selling land, brought within twelve years of the right accruing (19(1)) |
