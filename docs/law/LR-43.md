# Auctioneers Rules (LN 120 of 1997)

The law register's row is [LR-43](../regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Auctioneers Rules, Legal Notice No. 120 of 1997 |
| Regulator | The Auctioneers Licensing Board; a court where it directs otherwise |
| Also called | Auctioneers Rules |
| Made under | The Auctioneers Act (Cap. 526), s. 30 |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | How a licensed auctioneer repossesses, stores, advertises and sells property, with the notice periods for each step |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| reg 5 | Part III, on attaching and selling property, applies to repossession under a third party's letter of instruction, unless another written law provides otherwise or a court directs (5(1)); the letter is in Sale Form 1 (5(2)) |
| reg 7 | The debtor pays the auctioneer's charges, unless the debtor cannot be found, has no goods, or the proceeds do not cover them, when the creditor pays |
| reg 8 | The auctioneer keeps repossessed goods safe and insured until they are sold or the repossession is withdrawn (8(1)) |
| reg 9 | Where resistance, intimidation or a breach of the peace is likely, the auctioneer asks for a police escort (9(1)) |
| reg 10 | Before the sale, the debtor may ask a court for an independent valuation |
| reg 12 | For movables other than perishables and livestock: the instruction recorded (12(1)(a)); a proclamation listing each item's value and condition, signed by the owner or an adult at the premises (12(1)(b)); seven days' written notice in Sale Form 3 to redeem by paying the amount (12(1)(c)); only then removal to safe premises (12(1)(d), (g)); an advertisement within seven days of removal, and the sale at least seven and at most fourteen days after the first newspaper advertisement (12(1)(f)) |
| reg 16 | The advertisement gives the date, time and place, the conditions of sale, when to view, a description of the goods and whether there is a reserve price (16(1)); in a newspaper, unless a court orders otherwise (16(2)) |
| reg 17 | Goods repossessed under a contract or law are auctioned between 10.00 a.m. and 6.00 p.m. (17(1)(a)), at a venue open to the public (17(1)(c)); the highest bidder buys, on the conditions of sale (17(4)) |
| reg 18 | The auctioneer pays the proceeds, less charges, to the instructing party with an itemised account within fifteen days of selling movables (18(4)) |
