# Hire-Purchase Act (Cap. 507)

The law register's row is [LR-42](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Hire-Purchase Act (Cap. 507) |
| Regulator | None: the courts apply it |
| Also called | Hire-Purchase Act; Hire Purchase Act |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | How goods let on hire purchase are contracted, and when and how the owner may take them back |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 13 | The hirer may complete the purchase by paying the net balance, during the agreement or within twenty-eight days after the owner takes the goods back, with the costs of taking, storing and repairing them and interest due (13(3)) |
| s. 15 | Once two-thirds of the hire-purchase price is paid, the owner may recover the goods only by suit (15(1)); taking them otherwise ends the agreement, releases the hirer and lets the hirer and any guarantor recover all they paid (15(2)) |
| s. 16 | While such a suit runs, the owner enforces payment only by claiming it in the suit (16(1)) |
