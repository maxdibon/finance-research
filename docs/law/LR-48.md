# Consumer Protection Act (Cap. 501)

The law register's row is [LR-48](../governance/regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Citation | The Consumer Protection Act (Cap. 501), formerly No. 46 of 2012 |
| Regulator | None of its own: the courts, and the Kenya Consumers Protection Advisory Committee it sets up |
| Also called | Consumer Protection Act |
| Made under | An Act of Parliament |
| In force | Yes |
| What it is | Protection of consumers in their dealings with suppliers; Part VII governs credit agreements, and a lender's licence application states how it complies with it (LR-03 reg 6(2)(f)) |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| s. 56 | A borrower who receives none of the statements this Part requires owes no cost of borrowing, and never more than the statements disclosed |
| s. 58 | A borrower required to insure may buy the insurance from any lawful insurer, the lender refusing one only on reasonable grounds; a lender offering to arrange it says so in writing |
| s. 60 | An invitation to defer a payment states whether interest accrues meanwhile, and at what rate; otherwise that interest is waived |
| s. 61 | No default charges but reasonable legal costs of collecting, reasonable costs of realising or protecting a security, and reasonable charges the agreement allows for a dishonoured payment |
| s. 62 | A borrower may pay the whole balance at any time without a prepayment charge or penalty, and is refunded or credited the unearned part of the cost of borrowing |
| s. 63 | Representations about a credit agreement only as prescribed |
| s. 65 | An initial disclosure statement delivered at or before the time the borrower enters the agreement, with the prescribed information |
