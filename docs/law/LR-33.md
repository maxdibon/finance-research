The law register's row is [LR-33](../regulation.md); this card says what
a reader of any of our documents needs to know about it, once (FS-868).

|---|---|

| In force | Yes |
| What it is | How deposit-taking microfinance banks are licensed and run: governance, capital, lending, records, audit and reporting |

## Provisions we cite

| Provision | What it requires |
|---|---|
| reg 37 | Customers properly identified, and records of customers, their sources of funds and their transactions kept for at least seven years; the institution satisfied that it deals with real persons and those empowered to act |
| reg 40 | An annual internal audit plan; the internal auditors' reports and working papers kept for at least five years (40(4)) |
