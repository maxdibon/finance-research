The document vault has its own spec. It sets nine rules: every document carrying personal data is kept there, checked and scanned on arrival, never changed, and encrypted under the institution's keys. Every read is logged. Documents go out only as a link, and each is deleted when its retention ends, with its replicas and backups. The spec also covers how documents arrive, the vault's interfaces and events, who sees which documents, storage and recovery, threats, and ten acceptance tests. One question needs a decision: a signed document kept in both the vault and Odoo.
