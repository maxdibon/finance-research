The back-office spec now shows how more than one ERP, or more than one version of one, runs: institutions each on their own edition through one gateway, two products sharing the jobs in one instance, and old and new side by side during a port or an upgrade, with one live edition per job throughout. Falling back is safe until staff are let into the new one; the upgrade runbook now says so.
