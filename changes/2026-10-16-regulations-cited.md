Every law whose provisions we cite has a card in docs/law/: its citation,
regulator, what it was made under, whether it is in force, and what each
cited provision requires, read from the law's text. Documents that cite
provisions end with a "Regulations cited" section made from the cards,
and can print the same for a Word or PDF document read outside Dibon.
The lender rules, Roan Capital's product, the microfinance module and the
privacy register carry it first.
