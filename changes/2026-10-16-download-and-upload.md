The team's words are said from your side. Download brings the
repository's latest into your copy. Your work then goes out in steps:
save keeps it on your computer, upload puts it on your own branch in the
repository, share puts it before the team for review, and release, the
owner's, takes it to the public. "Update" could mean either way, so it
is answered with a question: download or upload?
