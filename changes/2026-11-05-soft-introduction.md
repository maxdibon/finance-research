The CBK licence pack now covers what the licence application itself will carry, from the regulations' own lists: a licence (reg 6) or a registration (reg 9). It also has three more templates: statements on the Consumer Protection Act and the Competition Act, a description of the delivery channels (also the substance of CBK's channel notice), and a submission index of what goes now and what follows. Roan Capital's soft launch on 30 November, with its current process run in parallel for about a month, is in its deployment.
